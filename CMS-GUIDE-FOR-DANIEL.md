# How to Update Your FixWell Website

Your website can be updated at:
**https://www.fixwellservices.co.uk/keystatic**

## First Time Login

1. Go to the link above
2. Click **"Log in with GitHub"**
3. Sign in with the GitHub account you were invited on, and authorise access
4. You're in!

*(If you don't have a GitHub account yet, your developer will send you an invite first — just let them know if you haven't received one.)*

## What You Can Update

- **Business Info & Contact Details** — phone number, email, address, opening hours, areas you cover
- **Overall Rating Display** — the link to your Google reviews page and the overall rating shown on the site
- **Certificate Numbers** — your Public Liability policy number and DBS certificate number
- **Testimonials** — customer reviews shown on the homepage
- **Work Gallery** — photos of completed jobs, with captions
- **Services & Info Pages** — the written content (intro, sections, FAQs) on each service and info page

## What You Can't Change (On Purpose)

Page titles, page web addresses, and how pages link to each other are locked in the site's code — changing these could break links or hurt how the site ranks on Google. If you ever need one of these changed, get in touch with your developer.

## Making a Change

1. Click into the section you want (e.g. "Testimonials")
2. Click on the entry you want to edit, or **Add** to create a new one
3. Make your changes in the form
4. Click **Save**

Every change you save updates the live website automatically, usually within a minute or two.

## A Few Notes

- **Text fields are plain text, not rich text** — if you need bold text or a link added somewhere new, let your developer know rather than typing formatting symbols, as they won't display correctly.
- On the **Services & Info Pages** section, you'll see a field called "Page identifier" at the top of each entry — leave that one alone, it's used internally to match the page to its web address.
- If something looks wrong after saving, don't worry — nothing is lost. Every change is recorded, so it can always be undone. Just get in touch.

## Getting Help

If you're ever unsure about a change, or something doesn't look right after saving, reach out before making further edits.
