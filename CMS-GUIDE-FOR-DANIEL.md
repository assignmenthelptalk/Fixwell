# Editing Your Website — A Quick Guide

You can now update most of the content on your website yourself, without needing to know any code. This guide walks you through it.

## Logging In

1. Go to `yoursite.co.uk/admin` (replace with your actual website address)
2. Log in with the email invitation you received
3. You'll land on a dashboard with a list of things you can edit

## What You Can Edit

- **Business Info** — your business details, contact info, hours
- **Reviews Settings** — how reviews are displayed on your site
- **Trust Badge Certificates** — your Public Liability policy number, DBS certificate number, etc.
- **Testimonials** — customer reviews shown on your site
- **Work Gallery** — the photos showing examples of your work
- **Services & Info Pages** — the main text content on your service and information pages (intros, sections, FAQs)

## What You Can't Edit (On Purpose)

Page titles, page addresses (URLs), and how pages link to each other are locked in the site's code. This protects the site's structure and search engine ranking — accidentally changing these could break links or hurt how the site shows up in Google. If you ever need one of these changed, just get in touch with your developer.

## Making a Change

1. Click into the section you want to update (e.g. "Testimonials")
2. Click on the specific entry you want to edit, or "New" to add one
3. Make your changes in the form fields
4. Click **Publish** (or **Save** then **Publish**, depending on the section)

That's it — publishing sends the update live. Your website will automatically refresh with the new content, usually within a couple of minutes.

## A Few Notes

- **Text fields are plain text, not rich text** — if you need something bold or a link added, let your developer know rather than trying to add formatting yourself, as it won't display correctly.
- **Trust badge certificate numbers** are currently blank — add your Public Liability policy number and DBS certificate number when you have them, and they'll appear automatically on the site.
- If something looks wrong after publishing, don't panic — nothing is lost. Get in touch and it can be fixed or rolled back.

## Getting Help

If you're ever unsure about a change, or something doesn't look right after publishing, reach out before making further edits.
